# Code of Conduct

Kavach exists to protect people who are often made to feel foolish for being targeted. The same
respect applies to everyone who works on it.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are learning.
- Critique code and ideas, never the person.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming a mistake was careless.
- Credit other people's work.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Sharing anyone's private information, including real scam messages that identify a victim.
- Publishing real OTPs, card numbers or account numbers, even as examples.

## Reporting

Raise concerns privately with the project maintainer through the GitHub repository. Reports are
handled confidentially.
